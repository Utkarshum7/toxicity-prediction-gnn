About the ATAD5 assay

ATAD5 (ATPase family AAA domain-containing protein 5) helps maintain genome stability by unloading the replication clamp PCNA from DNA after replication. The Tox21 SR-ATAD5 assay is a cell-based stress-response read-out related to genotoxic stress; the model here learns to predict that single assay's outcome from molecular structure. Activity in one in-vitro assay does not establish toxicity in humans.
